# RAM Pain Clinic — Pixlie Motion Brief

## Goal
Create a 6-second, vertical 9:16 cinematic logo intro for RAM Pain Clinic using the supplied RAM `R` logo as the exact final geometry.

## Pixlie workflow
Image-to-video (`i2v`), 6 seconds, 9:16.

## Master prompt
Use the RAM Pain Clinic logo as the visual anchor. Begin with luminous ribbon-like strands in the RAM brand palette tangled into a dense, confused knot, twisting around each other in a dark futuristic medical-tech space. During the first seconds, the ribbons gradually organize, untangle and resolve into the sharp geometric strokes of the RAM R logo. The camera performs a smooth cinematic orbit around the moving strands with pronounced but elegant zoom-in and zoom-out motion, subtle parallax, volumetric light, glass-like reflections and precise neon edges. The environment feels scientific, premium, abstract, medical and technological rather than cyberpunk or gaming. Finish with the exact original RAM R logo centered in a clean hero frame, with stable proportions, crisp edges, controlled glow and a subtle sense of physical depth. No text, no people, no extra symbols, no additional letters, no anatomy, no floating UI, no deformation of the final logo.

## Three-shot structure
1. **0.0–2.0 s — Entangled state:** Extreme close camera begins inside a dense knot of luminous ribbons. The strands twist, overlap and orbit like a confused neural bundle. Strong spatial depth, dark glassy environment, soft volumetric haze. Camera begins a fast but smooth arc.
2. **2.0–4.5 s — Resolution:** The tangled ribbons progressively separate and align into the exact strokes of the R. Camera completes a controlled 360° orbit while zooming out. Motion slows as geometry becomes recognizable. No sudden morphs; transformation must feel physically continuous.
3. **4.5–6.0 s — Hero lock:** The exact RAM R logo locks into place, centered. Camera eases to a near-static hero angle. Neon edge light and a subtle shadow reveal the mass and depth of the logo. End cleanly with no text.

## Negative prompt
cartoon, anime, playful, childish, gaming logo, cyberpunk city, humans, doctors, patients, hands, extra letters, typography, text overlays, icons, medical cross, anatomy, brain, spine, distorted R, warped logo, melted geometry, duplicated logo, noisy particles, excessive sparks, lens dirt, shaky camera, jump cuts, abrupt morph, low resolution, oversaturated colors, flat 2D logo, incorrect proportions

## Quality constraints
- The final R silhouette must match the supplied source logo exactly.
- Keep the logo centered in the final 1.5 seconds.
- Prefer physically plausible ribbon motion and smooth easing.
- Preserve a dark luxury medical-tech mood and RAM brand palette.
- Render at the highest available quality; use 4K export when available.
